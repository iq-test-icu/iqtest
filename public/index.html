About this test

IQ·Test is a 16-item cognitive skills self-assessment split evenly across four categories: numeric reasoning, verbal reasoning, logical deduction, and pattern recognition. It takes most people 4–6 minutes. The free result shows your raw score and category split immediately; the paid report adds an estimated percentile and a short written analysis of your reasoning pattern.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a real IQ test?

No. This is a self-insight quiz for entertainment and personal reflection, not a clinical or psychometrically validated intelligence test. Scores here shouldn't be used for diagnostic, employment, or educational decisions — for that, consult a licensed psychologist.

How is the score calculated?

Your raw score across the 16 items is converted to a cognitive index on an 85–145 scale, modeled on a standard mean-100 / SD-15 distribution, as a familiar frame of reference rather than a clinical claim.

What's the difference between Basic and Detailed?

Basic ($1.99) unlocks your full 4-category percentile breakdown. Detailed ($3.99) adds a written reasoning analysis plus the historical figure whose estimated score lands closest to yours. Both arrive by email in about 30 seconds.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — every report email includes a self-serve refund link valid for 7 days. No support ticket required.
