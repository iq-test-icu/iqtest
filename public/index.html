Frequently Asked Questions

What does this test measure?
This assessment measures fluid intelligence (Gf) across four cognitive domains: verbal reasoning, numerical reasoning, logical deduction, and spatial visualization. It uses 16 original items calibrated with Item Response Theory to estimate your cognitive ability.
How is my score calculated?
Your responses are scored server-side using a Three-Parameter Logistic (3PL) Item Response Theory model. This accounts for item difficulty, discrimination, and guessing probability to produce a calibrated ability estimate, converted to a standard IQ scale (mean 100, standard deviation 15).
