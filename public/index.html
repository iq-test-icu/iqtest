About this test

IQ·Test is a 16-item cognitive skills self-assessment split evenly across four categories: numeric reasoning, verbal reasoning, logical deduction, and pattern recognition. It takes most people 4–6 minutes. The free result shows your raw score and category split immediately; the paid report adds an estimated percentile and a short written analysis of your reasoning pattern.
