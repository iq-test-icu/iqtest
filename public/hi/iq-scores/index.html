Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard deviation on modern IQ tests?
Most contemporary clinical assessments (such as the WAIS-IV) use a standard deviation of 15 points with a mean of 100.
What percentile represents a score of 130?
An IQ of 130 represents the 97.72nd percentile (top 2.28% of the population), meeting the entrance threshold for Mensa.
