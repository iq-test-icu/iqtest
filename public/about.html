About IQ·Test

IQ·Test is a short reasoning assessment built to be genuinely useful for self-reflection, not to sell you a premium subscription or a coaching programme. You take 16 questions, get your free score immediately, and if you want the deeper breakdown, you pay a flat fee for a one-time report.

The test covers four areas: number patterns, verbal reasoning, logical deduction, and pattern recognition. Your score is mapped to the same 85–145 reference scale used in traditional cognitive assessment so the number is familiar and readable, not arbitrary.

We are transparent about what this is and what it is not. This is not a clinical IQ test. It will not diagnose anything. It is a well-constructed quiz that gives you an honest look at how you process information, compared against a meaningful reference.

The historical matching

The Detailed Report identifies the historical figure whose documented cognitive profile most closely resembles yours. This is based on published biographical and academic records of how those individuals approached problems, not guesswork. Da Vinci, Curie, Tesla, Newton, and others are represented in the matching set.
