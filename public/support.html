Common questions

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If you're not satisfied with your report for any reason, email us within 7 days of purchase and we will refund you in full. No questions, no forms.

My report email went to spam. What should I do?

Mark it as "not spam" and add report@iq-test.icu to your contacts so future emails arrive correctly. Then email us and we'll resend.

I entered the wrong email address. Can you fix it?

Yes. Email support@iq-test.icu with your correct address and proof of purchase (a screenshot of the Stripe receipt is fine) and we will reroute your report.

How long does the report take to arrive?

Typically under 60 seconds after payment confirms. If it's been longer than 5 minutes, check spam or contact us.
