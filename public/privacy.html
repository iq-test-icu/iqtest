Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We wrote this to be readable, not just legally defensible. Here is exactly what we do with your information.

What we collect

When you take the test, we record only your answers and the time taken per question. We do not collect your name or any identifying information at this stage.

If you purchase a report, we collect your email address to deliver it. Your payment is processed entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not add you to any mailing list without a clear, separate opt-in. Your email is used to send your report and nothing else unless you have explicitly asked for updates.

How long we keep it

Test session data is anonymised after 90 days. Email addresses associated with a purchase are retained for 12 months for support purposes, then deleted. You can request deletion at any time by writing to privacy@iq-test.icu.

Third-party services

We use Stripe for payments (their privacy policy applies to payment processing), Resend to deliver emails, and Supabase to store session data securely. None of these providers receive your data for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. The only storage we use is a session token in your browser's local storage so your test progress is not lost if you refresh the page. It is deleted when you close the tab.

Your rights

You can request a copy of any data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email privacy@iq-test.icu and we will respond within 5 business days.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, email us at privacy@iq-test.icu. We are a small team and we read every message.
