# Search Engine Landscape & Reachability Verdicts

## zh (Simplified Chinese)
- **Dominant Search Engine:** Baidu (Mainland China), Google / Bing (Overseas & Diaspora).
- **Reachability Verdict:** Mainland Baidu organic is out of reach without ICP license and mainland hosting. Target overseas Mandarin speakers in Singapore, Malaysia, North America, and diaspora via Google and Bing. Do not add Baidu verification tags or mainland Baidu sitemaps.

## ko (Korean)
- **Dominant Search Engine:** Naver (~60%), Google (~35%).
- **Reachability Verdict:** Naver heavily favors native Blog/Cafe properties. Web search traffic captured primarily via Google Korea. Expect moderate organic growth.

## ja (Japanese)
- **Dominant Search Engine:** Google (Yahoo! Japan search results are powered by Google).
- **Reachability Verdict:** Standard Google optimization applies fully. High addressable search volume for IQ benchmark queries.

## ar (Arabic)
- **Dominant Search Engine:** Google.
- **Reachability Verdict:** High search volume across GCC and MENA. Requires proper RTL layout rendering and Western Arabic numerals.

## hi (Hindi)
- **Dominant Search Engine:** Google.
- **Reachability Verdict:** Large user base; queries often mix Devanagari script with Latin technical keywords (Hinglish). Technical terms must remain recognizable.

## de, fr, es, pt, it, nl, tl (Western & Southeast Asian Markets)
- **Dominant Search Engine:** Google (>90% market share).
- **Reachability Verdict:** Standard Google technical SEO and entity graph mapping apply with high direct reach.
